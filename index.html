PageSpeed Sampler

Finds a site's sitemap, groups the URLs into collection types, samples up to 10 pages per collection (100 max) with key pages pinned, measures each one with the Google PageSpeed Insights API, then averages the results. Add up to four sites to benchmark a client against its competitors, page role by page role. Runs entirely in your browser.
