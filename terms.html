What this is

PageSpeed Sampler is a free, open-source utility published by Jake Labate, SEO Consultant. It discovers a site's sitemap, samples a set of URLs, measures them with the Google PageSpeed Insights API, and reports the results. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no subscription and no service being operated on your behalf.

Your credentials, your quota

The tool uses your own Google PageSpeed Insights API key and, optionally, your own Google OAuth client. All API usage counts against your quota and is subject to Google's own terms. You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure and for any usage incurred under them.

Acceptable use

  • Measure sites you own, sites you are engaged to work on, or sites that are publicly accessible and that you have a legitimate professional reason to benchmark.
  • Do not use the tool to place unreasonable load on any site, to circumvent access controls, or in any way that breaks the law or the terms of the sites you are measuring.
  • Requests are made from your browser under your own network identity. What you do with the tool is attributable to you, not to its author.
